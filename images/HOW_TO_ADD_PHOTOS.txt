HOW TO ADD YOUR PHOTOS
======================

1. Save your 5 photos with EXACTLY these filenames:
      photo1.jpg   (bar / drinking beer photo)
      photo2.jpg   (dark car selfie – blue light in glasses)
      photo3.jpg   (dark car selfie – red light)
      photo4.jpg   (dark car – Peppa Pig sticker)
      photo5.jpg   (tilted dark car selfie)

2. Put all files in this /images/ folder.

3. To change stories, dates, or names: open js/app.js and edit:
      - CONFIG  (your names and anniversary date)
      - PHOTOS  (date, title, story, location for each photo)

4. To add MORE photos later, copy one of the photo objects in the
   PHOTOS array (in js/app.js) and add a new entry — that's it.

That's all you need! No extra software required.
